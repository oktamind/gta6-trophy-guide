The road to the platinum

Five stages, in the order that wastes the least replay. The game has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so the save habits in stage 1 protect everything after it. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they switch trophies off.

  1. Stage 1 Story first, save smart

    Chapters 1 to 4 and the finale. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot before Chapter 1 ends — it is the one missable trophy in the set.

  2. Stage 2 Strangers & partners

    Eight stranger strands across Leonida, the five named stranger finales, and the trophies you earn with your partner beside you. Start every strand you find; only finished strands count.

  3. Stage 3 Activities & skill

    Races, drone races, the shooting range, stunts, fishing, arcade games, bowling and challenges. Long grinds, not hard ones — fold them into your travelling instead of grinding each in one sitting.

  4. Stage 4 Collectibles & exploration

    Card sets, WAiNK scans, vehicle discovery, meditation spots and all 52 areas of Leonida. Travel everywhere with the WAiNK app open and the count climbs on its own.

  5. Stage 5 Money, robberies & cleanup

    Bank vaults, jewelry stores, armored trucks, gang operations, marked bills, garages — then the in-game 100% checklist, and the platinum follows.

How this guide was made

Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes it, as reported from Exophase and cross-checked with PSNProfiles on 18 November 2026. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry and names its sources beside it. Seven trophies carry an unconfirmed at launch note: no launch-week source confirms a method for them yet, and this guide says so instead of inventing one. Counts used in the guides — 250 stunts, 100 vehicles, 100 WAiNK locations, 50 challenges, 3 card sets, 8 stranger strands, 52 areas, 18 meditation spots, 14 drone-race locations, 42 shooting-range challenges, 8 garages, 9 activity types — come from the official descriptions and the launch-week guides cited beside each trophy.

Sources cited throughout: Exophase and PSNProfiles (the official list), PowerPyx, Planet GTA 6, Output Lag, KeenGamer, Press-Start, EGamers, All.gg, U7BUY and GTA Vice City Hub. Links open only when you follow them; this page fetches nothing over the network.
